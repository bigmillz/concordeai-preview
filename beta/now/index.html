    Free up disk space on the server

    Over SSH with your key. Reads run on their own; anything that writes waits for you.

    1. readdf -h /
      Filesystem      Size  Used Avail Use% Mounted on
/dev/nvme0n1p2  468G  441G  4.1G  99% /
    2. readsudo du -xh /var --max-depth=2 | sort -h | tail -3
      18G   /var/log/journal
19G   /var/log
26G   /var

      The system journal holds most of it.

    writeNeeds your approval
    sudo journalctl --vacuum-size=500M

    Keeps the newest 500 MB of the system journal and deletes older entries. Frees about 17 GB; nothing outside the journal is touched.
